2. For which characteristic do some offspring have traits in between Skye’s and Poppy’s traits? Explain. (For example, in some plants, a cross between a red- and white-flower plant will give pink flower offspring. This is called incomplete dominance.)

Nose length, tail style, and spikes. Nose length can be medium if there is a heterozygous genotype. Tail style can be either curly or straight with a heterozygous genotype, and spikes can be a combination of colors and numbers depending on what alleles are taken from each parent.

3. For which characteristic do some offspring have both Skye’s and Poppy’s traits? Explain. (For example, in humans, a person with type A blood and a person with type B blood can have a child with type AB blood. This is called co-dominance, as both traits appear in the offspring.)
Spikes, because each parent can pass their color or number.

5. Consider the pattern for sex determination.
a. How is a critter’s sex determined?

X and Y chromosomes. XY means male, YY means female.

b. Whose genetic contribution—Ocean’s or Lucy’s—determines the sex of the offspring?

Both, just like any other genotype. 100% of the chromosomes cannot come from one parent.
